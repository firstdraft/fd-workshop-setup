WORKSHOP LAPTOP SETUP (Windows)
===============================

1. Right-click the zip file you downloaded and choose "Extract All...".
   Extract it to your Documents folder.

2. Open Claude Desktop, go to the Code tab, and open the extracted
   "workshop-kit" folder.

3. Type:   Set up my laptop for the workshop

4. Claude will guide you. Your laptop may restart once or twice.
   After each restart: open Claude, open this same folder, and type:  continue

Before you start:
- Plug in your charger.
- Have your phone nearby (you may need it to look something up while
  the laptop restarts).
- Save and close other work.

If Claude is not working, double-click "Setup WSL without Claude.cmd" instead.
